## 5. Standards for every article

Use the same standard as the NIDA routine article:

1. One primary keyword in the SEO title, H1, first 100 words and one H2. Title tag 60 characters or fewer, meta description about 155 or fewer.
2. Answer-first summary at the top, table of contents, FAQ of 4 to 6 real questions.
3. 3 to 5 internal links to products (use the new Polish product titles) and 2 to 3 to other Poradnik posts. Always link the pillar.
4. Claims only from the manufacturer's product descriptions or INCI lists. No promised results and no medical claims. Retinoid and azelaic content needs a pregnancy and sensitivity note. PDRN needs the fish allergy note.
5. A named author with a short bio (currently the byline is "Zespół Luora"). Google's quality guidance favours a visible author.
6. A featured image with Polish alt text, and a "Polecane produkty" block at the end.
7. After publishing, add a link to the new post from the related existing posts.

## 6. What this plan does NOT have (be honest about it)

- **No Polish search volumes or difficulty scores.** The tools available in this session are US-only search plus a trends scan. The keywords above are hypotheses from trend evidence and catalogue fit, not measured demand.
- **Validation step:** connect Google Search Console (or DataForSEO) and, after 4 to 6 weeks of data, re-rank this list by real impressions. Move articles up or down accordingly.
- **No traffic targets.** There is no baseline, so any number would be made up. Measure instead: indexed articles, impressions and clicks per article in Search Console, clicks from articles to product pages, and sales of the NIDA and EPII bundles.
- Exosome and microneedle content stays on the watch list until there are products to link.
